## 🧮 What is GN Math?

**GN Math (Games & Numbers Math)** is a hand-picked, mobile-first collection of the best **free math games** on the web — from mental-math speed drills and times-tables trainers to algebra puzzles and geometry builders. Every link is tested for **mobile compatibility**, so games run instantly in your browser with no install, no flash, and no paywalls.

Whether you call them *mobile math games*, *unblocked math games for school*, or *cool math games on your phone* — this is your one-stop hub. 🎯

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Is everything really free?**
A: Yes. We only link to free HTML5 games and education portals. No downloads, no trials.

**Q: Do the games work on school Wi-Fi?**
A: We prioritize school-safe, unblocked education domains. Availability depends on your school's filter — most linked portals are commonly allowlisted.

**Q: Do I need to install anything?**
A: No. Everything plays in the browser. The optional PWA install just adds a home-screen icon.

**Q: Can I add my own game?**
A: Absolutely — read [docs/CONTRIBUTING.md](docs/CONTRIBUTING.md) and open a PR. Games must be free, mobile-friendly, and school-safe.

**Q: What does "GN" stand for?**
A: **G**ames & **N**umbers — our tagline: *Good games make good numbers.*
